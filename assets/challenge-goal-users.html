The Challenge

Seventeen Countries, Seventeen Ways of Handling the Same Request

Credit, return and exchange ran differently in every region, shaped by local policies, systems and hand-offs. Most decisions waited on a manual approval, and neither customers nor agents could predict what would happen next.

The Goal

One Framework That Works Everywhere, and Adapts Locally

Design a global CRE framework that automates routine decisions, gives people clear and predictable status, and still respects each country's policies, logistics and timelines.

Who We Designed For
CustomersWant a quick, predictable resolution and to know what happens next.
Care AgentsCreate and progress requests, today across several disconnected tools.
Operations & FinanceApprove credits, process refunds and recover returned stock.
Regional LeadsOwn local policies, margins and carrier relationships.
What Success Looks Like
  • Customers get faster, predictable resolutions
  • Agents take fewer steps and wait on fewer approvals
  • The business tunes one rule model per country, not seventeen processes
  • Impact is proven in a pilot before a global rollout
