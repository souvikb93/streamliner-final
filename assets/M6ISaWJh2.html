How We Worked Together

01Playback & Reframe

We played back the journey maps, key findings, and three insights, then turned each opportunity area into How Might We questions for the group to explore and vote on.

Output: Prioritised HMWs per opportunity area
02Concept Sprint

Mixed teams explored ideas for the end-to-end request lifecycle. We discussed different approaches, weighed trade-offs, and grouped concepts by stage until a structured framework emerged.

Output: Five-module lifecycle, from Create & Edit to Disposition & Recovery
03Feasibility & Rules

I defined the business rules for Verify & Review and how requests should move across regions and systems. I then worked with enterprise architects to validate the rules against technical feasibility and regional requirements.

Output: Final Verify & Review rule set and regional integration touchpoints

What We Decided Together

Shared Core, Regional Rules

One standard process across all countries, with local rules and exceptions layered on top.

Five Modules, One Case

Credit, Return and Exchange follow the same structure and share a common CRE case.

Clear Automation Scope

Automate routine steps and status updates to reduce manual effort and handovers.

Verify & Review Rule Set

Defined the rules and checks for Verify & Review, then validated them with enterprise architects.

These decisions became the brief for Streamliner: five modules around one standard CRE case.
