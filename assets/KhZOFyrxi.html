Streamliner · CRE lifecycle
1

Create & Edit

Captures Credit, Return, and Exchange requests, pulls order and product details, and validates policy rules into a standard CRE case.

2

Verify & Review

Checks eligibility and applies business policies to approve, reject, or flag a request for review.

3

Return Method

Selects the right logistics method and generates shipping instructions for the return or exchange.

4

Process & Track

Runs return operations across enterprise systems with status visibility and a full CRE history.

5

Disposition & Recovery

Handles inspection outcomes, refunds, replacements, and inventory recovery after the item arrives.

Every module integrates with Dell's enterprise systems. Regions share the structure; only the business rules change.
